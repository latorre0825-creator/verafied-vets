**Description:**

You served your country. You should not have to pay anyone to understand the benefits you earned.

VeRaFIED Vets is a free app from VeRaFIED Studio, veterans helping veterans, that walks you through VA benefits in plain language. No fees. No ads. We never sell your data.

- Benefits Screener: see what you may qualify for in five questions.
- DD214 Help: store it safely, or learn how to get a free copy.
- Condition Tracker: write down your health problems and see what evidence is missing.
- Ask Vera: our AI helper answers your benefits questions in simple words.
- Free Human Help: connect with accredited Veterans Service Officers.
- Scam Shield: learn how to spot people who profit from veterans.
- Get Help Now: Veterans Crisis Line, homeless veterans hotline, and VA numbers in one tap.

Built for every veteran, including veterans who are homeless or on the move. Works offline and in low-data mode. Large text and dark mode.

VeRaFIED Vets is not affiliated with the Department of Veterans Affairs. The VA makes all final decisions. Vera is an AI helper, not a lawyer, doctor, or the VA.
